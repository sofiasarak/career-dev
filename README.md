# EDS Job Market Analysis

For my position as the Career Development Student Assistant at the [Bren School](https://bren.ucsb.edu/), I compile and analyze data on the environmental data science (EDS) job market. These data get synthetized in quarterly reports (developed as .qmd files), which can be found in the `quarterly report qmds` folder. Exploratory visualizations – that contributed to deliverables such as an [infographic](https://sofiasarak.github.io/blog/career-dev-report/) and my final presentation to the Master of Environmental Data Science curriculum committee can be found in the `visualization` folder. 

Data for this analysis comes from my own collection of environmental data science positions, and is included (by quarter) in the `data` folder.
